DiaFitus — client collage photos
================================

Drop client/lifestyle photos in this folder and they'll appear in the
collage on the homepage (right side of the hero).

 - Accepted formats: .jpg .jpeg .png .webp
 - Recommended size: ~600x800 portrait, under 300 KB each
 - Up to 9 photos shown. Name them anything (e.g. client1.jpg).
 - If this folder is empty, the homepage shows tidy colored placeholders.
