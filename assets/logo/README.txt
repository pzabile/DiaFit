DiaFitus — brand assets
=======================

These are SVG placeholders that match the brand concept (blue water drop,
flexing figure, green leaf, glucose monitor). They scale infinitely and
look sharp on any device.

To swap them for your designer-made PNG/SVG:

  - logo-mark.svg  (or logo-mark.png)   — symbol only, used everywhere
                                           the brand appears (nav bars,
                                           sidebars, success / receipt
                                           cards, emails, favicon)
  - logo-full.svg  (or logo-full.png)   — symbol + 'DiaFitus' wordmark.
                                           Used on legal pages and the
                                           order receipt.

If you upload PNG versions, name them logo-mark.png and logo-full.png and
the templates will pick them up automatically (they prefer .png when both
exist).

Recommended sizes:
  - logo-mark   : 512 x 512 PNG, transparent background
  - logo-full   : 1200 x 360 PNG, transparent background
  - favicon     : 256 x 256 PNG (we generate a .svg favicon already,
                  but iOS Safari prefers .png)
